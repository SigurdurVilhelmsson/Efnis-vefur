The Icelandic Chemical Society organizes chemistry conferences with a semi-regular interval. Here on the website you can find information about [past conferences](/radstefnur/) as well as the upcoming conference, which will be the [9th Efnís conference](/en/radstefnur/2022/), to be held August 11th-12th.
